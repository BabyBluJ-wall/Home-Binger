# Trademark Guidelines & Policy

**Home Binger™**, **The CordCut Co-op™**, **TCC™**, and **BluJ Productions** are trademarks and brand identities owned by BluJ Productions.

These guidelines explain how you may and may not use our brand names, trademarks, and logos.

---

## 1. Our Philosophy: Open Code, Protected Brand

We believe strongly in software freedom, personal ownership, and privacy. The source code of **Home Binger™** is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)](LICENSE).

However, open-source licenses cover the **code**, not the **trademark or brand identity**. Our trademarks exist to ensure that users downloading software bearing the *Home Binger™* or *The CordCut Co-op™* names can trust that it originates directly from BluJ Productions and adheres to our privacy and security standards.

---

## 2. Permitted Uses (No Formal Permission Needed)

You may use our trademarks without prior written approval in the following non-commercial situations:

- **Truthful Factual Statements:** You may state truthfully that your addon, theme, script, or configuration is "compatible with Home Binger™" or "designed for The CordCut Co-op™".
- **Linking to the Project:** You may use the name to link directly to the official repository (https://github.com/BabyBluJ-wall/Home-Binger) or our community pages.
- **Community Discussion & Reviews:** You may use the names in tutorials, reviews, blog posts, videos, and articles discussing the project.

---

## 3. Prohibited Uses (Requires Rebranding)

To prevent confusion and protect users:

- **Forks and Modified Builds:** If you modify, rebuild, or redistribute the software publicly, you must **rebrand your build** under a different name and distinct logo. You may not name your fork "Home Binger", "Home Binger Extended", "The CordCut Co-op", or any confusingly similar variation.
- **False Endorsement / Affiliation:** You may not use our names or logos in a manner that suggests BluJ Productions sponsors, endorses, or is affiliated with your website, fork, or service.
- **Commercial Use:** You may not use our trademarks, brand names, or logos to sell goods, services, or paid hosting.
- **Domain Names & Social Handles:** You may not register domain names or social media accounts incorporating "HomeBinger" or "CordCutCoop" without permission.
