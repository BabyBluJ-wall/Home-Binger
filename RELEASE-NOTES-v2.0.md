# Home Binger 2.0 — the game room & watch-party graduation

**The big one.** A fourth room, three ways to watch together, keyless music, and a version number that finally makes sense.

## 🎮 The game room (the den)
Through the rising neon pocket door: a battle station with a glass showcase tower (hue-cycling fans, a glowing water block), a real RGB keyboard and mouse, and **three monitors that actually work** — the **Discord portal** (your servers and DMs, docked beside you in the desktop app), the **music station** (YouTube itself, keyless — mostly ad-free, honestly worded), and your **Steam library** (yours plus everything shared on your network). The store's glow-lit logo hangs above; the three-meter neon **angel-demon sign** — feathered wings, floating halo, devil horns, arrow-tipped tail — fills the store-side wall.

## 👀 Watch together — three ways
- **In the theater:** the host clicks Watch together and the room follows — drift-corrected sync that pauses while someone buffers (and a guest whose connection keeps struggling stops holding the room hostage after a few pauses: the party plays on, they catch up when it steadies).
- **Anywhere, from a plain link:** **Share this Home Binger — live** gives you a link and a six-character room code. Friends open it in ANY browser — phone counts — type a name, and watch your screen live with your system's sound. No install, no account, no VPN: the connection carries STUN plus a free relay fallback that crosses home routers and cellular. Quality tiers are capped so a share can't overdrive a thin uplink, the guest player smooths itself on rough links, and if a chat app mangles the link they just type the code.
- **App-to-app:** friends running their own Home Binger sync theater screens directly, peer-to-peer.

## 🖥️ Also in this release
- **The big screen takes links** — paste a YouTube link or playlist in the Guide's search and it plays ON the theater screen; full-screen Guide docking; CC on the theater remote.
- **The graphics dial** (Settings → My Theme) — Auto watches your frame rate and steps render resolution to fit the machine; weaker PCs stay smooth.
- **Photosensitive comfort switch** — calms every flashing effect; the game room's RGB and the dance floor drift softly instead of strobing.
- **Panels free the mouse**, the TV Guide can't stall, the den's hallway lip is gone, and a stack of real-network fixes (buffering on shares, the Error 153 embed break, desktop mouse capture).

## 📦 Getting it
- **Already running Home Binger?** Unzip this release anywhere near your old folder and open the new **HomeBinger.exe** — your accounts, users and settings migrate themselves automatically; the old folder gets marked "(old — you can delete this)". The app will also show you the "new version out" notice on its own.
- **New here?** Unzip, double-click **HomeBinger.exe** (SmartScreen → *More info → Run anyway* — the exe is unsigned), and stock the shelves from your Plex, Jellyfin, or any folder. A **1 - START HERE** file walks you through it in plain words.

## 🔢 The version scheme, going forward
This release is **2.0**. Updates and bug fixes will be **2.x**, and the library megaproject lands as **3.0**. (Why not 1.50? The last public release was v1.15, and "1.50" reads as "1.5" — which would place it *before* v1.6. 2.0 tells the truth: this is the big one.)
