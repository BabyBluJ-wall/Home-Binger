# Home Binger 2.1 — watch parties, sharper

**2.0's remote watch parties proved themselves in the field: a guest hundreds of miles away, barely any delay.** This update spends that proven headroom on the picture.

## 🎈 The quality tiers, re-tuned
- **Saver 720p30 ≈ 2 Mb/s · Balanced 900p60 ≈ 6 · High 1080p60 ≈ 12** (was 1.2 / 3 / 4.5 — the old insurance caps wouldn't spend the bandwidth real links had)
- **NEW: Ultra — up to 1440p60 at ≈ 20 Mb/s**, the sharpest share on the menu. Ultra captures your window at its native resolution — it shines when the app fills a 1440p+ monitor.
- Still buffered-network friendly: the guest player's adaptive jitter buffer and the connection's own congestion control remain, so thin links adapt down instead of stuttering.

## 📊 Everything else
- The share panel shows all four tiers with the honest per-viewer upload cost before you pick
- Measured requirements now in the README: the app draws ~0.4–0.6 GB of memory; the install unpacks to ~330 MB
