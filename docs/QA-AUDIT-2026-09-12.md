## 4 · Live TV simulation — the big de-risk (design reversal recorded)

Rig: vendored hls.js 1.7.3 (Apache-2.0, 620 KB) + a minimal m3u8-rewrite
proxy (the §6 design, ~60 lines) + 12 real official-tier US channels
from iptv-org.

- **Direct play from a page origin: 0/12** — the stream URLs in the
  index are mostly third-party mirrors (jmp2.uk) that send no CORS.
  Only Akamai answered with CORS `*`. → the doc's "direct play" verdict
  is REVERSED.
- **Through the proxy: 9/12 PLAYED real frames** — all 8 Pluto channels
  in 0.5–3 s to first frames; the 3 Akamai ABC News streams 404'd at
  the variant level from a datacenter IP (geo/token edge behavior —
  the flakiness caveat, now measured; home IPs may differ).
- Probe bugs that became design rules: resolve playlist lines against
  the **post-redirect** URL; guard against double-responding on
  upstream errors.
- RESEARCH-LIVETV.md updated: **proxy = REQUIRED + PROVEN core design**,
  direct play = later optimization for CORS-open CDNs.
